# Role: Global Creative Director, Cinematographer & Senior Motion Engineer for 《算了 · Suanle》
# Target Model: Claude Opus 5.5 (Claude Motion & Video Prompt Engineering)

You are tasked with directing and developing the complete cinematic promotional launch video (Brand Manifesto Film) and interactive motion prototype for 《算了 · Suanle》—a next-generation Eastern philosophical healing and destiny-reconciliation application.

---

## 1. Executive Context & Brand Philosophy
- **Product Name**: 《算了 · Suanle》
- **Core Slogan**: “算一算，然后，算了。” (Calculate, and then, Let It Go.)
- **Live URL**: https://www.suanle.cc
- **Official Asset Repository**: https://github.com/Evan-Joseph/suanle/tree/main/promo
- **Philosophical Essence**: 
  Modern individuals seek divination not for superstitious fatalism, but to find psychological grounding amidst overwhelming uncertainty and burnout. 
  《算了》 is an insightful, gentle old friend who understands BaZi (Four Pillars), Ziwei Doushu, I-Ching (Six Lines), and Tarot, but understands the human heart even better.
  - “算” (Calculate): To untangle mental knots, perceive boundaries, and observe the natural ebb and flow of life.
  - “算了” (Let It Go): To release futile obsession, embrace reality with radical acceptance (ACT therapy), and step forward unburdened.
- **Hero Interactive Features to Showcase**:
  1. **The Let-Go Paper Basket (纸篓心事)**: Writing raw anxieties on textured rice paper, crumpling it with real physical fiber wrinkles, throwing it into a woven bamboo basket with dynamic physics and acoustic bowl chime (磬音).
  2. **Three Bronze Coins I-Ching Casting (六爻摇卦)**: Three Kaiyuan Tongbao coins tumbling, spinning, and settling on a weathered tea table with crisp metallic clacks, casting the hexagram as a mirror to one's own inner clarity.
  3. **Fortune K-Line & Chart (八字紫微 & 运势K线)**: Visualizing life not as a flat line, but as rhythmic waves of accumulation, breakout, and consolidation.
  4. **The Sage Aphorism Card (水墨箴言长图)**: Generating exquisite Song-dynasty typographic cards with a red vermilion "算了" cinnabar seal.
- **Strict Creative Restrictions**:
  - **NO administrative backend UI or server metrics** (keep it pure humanistic art and product experience).
  - **NO defensive disclaimers or immersion-breaking buzzwords** (no "for entertainment purposes only", no corporate jargon).
  - **NO cheap flashing occult tropes** (no neon pentagrams, no cheesy lightning or EDM chants; strictly refined Song-dynasty wabi-sabi minimalism).

## 2. Production Deliverables Required From You

### PART 1: The Brand Manifesto Voiceover Script (文案台词脚本)
Write a powerful, resonant, 60-second voiceover script in Chinese. The tone must be intimate, deep, grounded, and empathetic—like a wise, weathered friend sharing hot tea on a rainy evening. Mark emotional rhythm, pauses (`...`), and voice pacing.

### PART 2: Master Shot-by-Shot Storyboard Table (工业级分镜总表)
Produce a comprehensive markdown production table with the following mandatory columns:
`[Shot #]` | `[Timecode]` | `[Framing & Camera Movement]` | `[Visual Composition & Lighting]` | `[Voiceover Sync]` | `[Foley & Sound Design]`
Cover all 60 seconds (Shot 01 to Shot 11), progressing from cold urban anxiety, through warm tea and tactile divination rituals, to final morning light and profound release.

### PART 3: Cinema-Grade Video Generation Prompts (Sora / Kling / Runway Gen-3)
For the 4 most pivotal keyframe shots (The Rainy Window, The Tumbling Bronze Coins, The Crumpled Paper Throw, The Vermilion Cinnabar Seal Imprint), write production-ready English prompts specifying:
- 35mm film emulation, lens focal length, aperture (f/1.4 - f/2.8), lighting direction, color temperature, frame rate (120fps slow-motion where relevant), camera motion vectors, and tactile micro-textures.

### PART 4: Code-Based Interactive Motion Experience (Claude Motion / Opus 5.5 Sandbox Artifact)
Explore your full sandbox capabilities! Build a complete, self-contained, interactive single-file prototype (HTML5 + Tailwind CSS + Canvas / Web Animations API / SVG) that visually demonstrates the core aesthetic and micro-interactions of 《算了》:
- An atmospheric, grain-textured Song-dynasty canvas with ink-wash breathing background;
- An interactive paper crumpling & coin tossing physics simulation;
- An animated reveal of the signature vermilion cinnabar seal (朱红“算了”印章) with subtle ink bleed;
- Typography styled with authentic Chinese serif and balanced breathing rhythm.
